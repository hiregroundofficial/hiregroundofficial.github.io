Everything you might want to know about The Hire Ground, what we do, who we're for, and how to get involved.

The Hire Ground is a student-led organization that helps students explore careers, understand the world of work, and build practical skills for their future careers.

The Hire Ground is mainly for high school and postsecondary students who want to explore different careers, build professional skills, and learn more about the workplace.

We provide career resources, career exploration tools, professional interviews, educational content, workshops, networking opportunities, mock interviews, and other hands-on learning opportunities.

The Career Readiness Quiz is a 25-question quiz designed to help you identify areas of career development where you may need more preparation. Your results can also point you toward The Hire Ground resources that may be useful to you.

The Career Search Tool lets you explore different careers and learn about things like education requirements, qualifications, income, work environment, job demand, skills, and other information about each career.

You can get involved by using our resources, completing the Career Readiness Quiz, exploring different careers, attending our events, following us on social media, sharing our content, or taking part in available opportunities.
