Technology

Software Developer

Software developers design, write, test, and maintain the computer programs and applications people use every day, from phone apps to banking systems. They usually work in teams, solving problems by breaking them down into steps a computer can follow.

$100,000/yr median Outlook: Very limited 4 years prep
View profile → Technology

Data Analyst

Data analysts turn raw numbers into useful information. They collect, clean, and study data, like sales figures or website traffic, and present their findings in charts and reports so organizations can make better decisions.

$85,000/yr median Outlook: Moderate 4 years prep
View profile → Technology

Cybersecurity Analyst

Cybersecurity analysts protect computer systems and data from hackers and attacks. They monitor networks for suspicious activity, respond to security incidents, and set up defenses to keep organizations' information safe.

$106,500/yr median Outlook: Limited 4 years prep
View profile → Creative & Design

UX Designer

UX (user experience) designers make apps and websites easy and enjoyable to use. They research what users need, sketch and test designs, and work with developers to turn ideas into products people love.

$68,000/yr median Outlook: Very limited 2–4 years prep
View profile → Technology

Web Developer

Web developers build and maintain websites, everything from online stores to news sites. They write the code that makes pages look right and work properly, connecting what users see with the data and services behind the scenes.

$83,000/yr median Outlook: Very limited 2–4 years prep
View profile → Technology

IT Support Specialist

IT support specialists are the tech problem-solvers people call when computers, software, or networks stop working. They diagnose issues, walk users through fixes, and keep workplaces running smoothly, often as the first line of technical help.

$68,500/yr median Outlook: Limited 2–3 years prep
View profile → Creative & Design

Graphic Designer

Graphic designers create the visuals that communicate ideas, logos, posters, packaging, social media graphics, and layouts for print and screens. They combine art, typography, and technology to make messages clear and memorable.

$68,000/yr median Outlook: Very limited 2–4 years prep
View profile → Technology

Network Administrator

Network administrators keep an organization's computer networks running, the systems that connect computers, servers, and the internet. They set up equipment, fix outages, protect against intrusions, and make sure everyone can work online without interruption.

$74,500/yr median Outlook: Moderate 2–3 years prep
View profile → Creative & Design

Digital Marketing Specialist

Digital marketing specialists promote brands and products online. They run social media campaigns, create ads for search engines, send email newsletters, and study data to figure out which strategies actually win customers.

$77,000/yr median Outlook: Limited 2–4 years prep
View profile → Healthcare

Registered Nurse

Registered nurses provide direct medical care to patients, from giving medications and monitoring vital signs to creating care plans and teaching patients about their health. They work in hospitals, clinics, long-term care homes, and community settings, and are the largest group of health care workers in Canada. About 134,400 people work in this occupation in Ontario.

$87,500/yr median Outlook: Very good 4 years prep
View profile → Healthcare

Family Physician

Family physicians are the first doctor most people see, they diagnose and treat everyday illnesses, manage chronic conditions like diabetes, provide preventive care and vaccinations, and refer patients to specialists when needed. About 31,350 work in Ontario, and roughly two-thirds are self-employed. Demand is expected to be strong, especially in rural and northern communities.

$234,000/yr median Outlook: Good 10+ years after high school prep
View profile → Healthcare

Surgeon

Surgeons diagnose conditions that need operations and perform surgery to treat injuries, disease, and deformities, from appendectomies to joint replacements. About 4,050 specialists in surgery work in Ontario, mostly in hospitals, and the work carries enormous responsibility since decisions are often life-or-death. It is one of the longest training paths in medicine.

$404,000/yr median Outlook: Good 13+ years after high school prep
View profile → Healthcare

Psychiatrist

Psychiatrists are medical doctors who diagnose and treat mental illnesses such as depression, anxiety, schizophrenia, and addiction. Unlike psychologists, psychiatrists can prescribe medication, and they often combine medication with psychotherapy. Note: Job Bank groups psychiatrists with all clinical and laboratory medical specialists (NOC 31100) for salary and outlook data below.

$296,000/yr median Outlook: Good 13+ years after high school prep
View profile → Healthcare

Pediatrician

Pediatricians are doctors who specialize in the health of babies, children, and teenagers, from treating ear infections and asthma to tracking growth and development and vaccinating. They work closely with worried parents, which takes patience and strong communication. Note: Job Bank groups pediatricians with all clinical and laboratory medical specialists (NOC 31100) for salary and outlook data below.

$296,000/yr median Outlook: Good 12+ years after high school prep
View profile → Healthcare

Pharmacist

Pharmacists make sure medications are safe and used correctly, they dispense prescriptions, check for dangerous drug interactions, counsel patients, and give vaccinations. About 17,350 pharmacists work in Ontario, mostly in community pharmacies and drug stores (76%) and hospitals (17%). Registration with the Ontario College of Pharmacists is required to practise.

$112,000/yr median Outlook: Good 6–7 years after high school prep
View profile → Healthcare

Physiotherapist

Physiotherapists help people recover from injuries, manage chronic pain, and improve movement through exercise programs, manual therapy, and rehabilitation techniques. About 14,850 work in Ontario, mostly in private clinics (62%) and hospitals (32%). About 40% are self-employed. You do not need a doctor's referral to see one in Ontario.

$95,500/yr median Outlook: Moderate 6–7 years after high school prep
View profile → Healthcare

Paramedic

Paramedics are the first medical responders to emergencies, they rush to 911 calls, treat patients at the scene, and transport them safely to hospital. About 11,600 people work in paramedical occupations in Ontario, mostly for ambulance services, and 92% work full-time. Ontario has three levels: Primary Care (PCP), Advanced Care (ACP), and Critical Care (CCP) paramedic.

$87,500/yr median Outlook: Good 2–3 years after high school prep
View profile → Healthcare

Dental Hygienist

Dental hygienists clean teeth, take dental x-rays, check for gum disease, and teach patients how to care for their teeth. About 14,650 work in Ontario, the vast majority in dental offices, and about one-third work part-time. In Ontario, dental hygienists can self-initiate treatment, and only CDHO registrants may legally use the title.

$91,500/yr median Outlook: Good 2–3 years after high school prep
View profile → Business & Finance

Accountant (CPA)

Accountants keep the financial records of businesses, governments, and non-profits accurate and honest. They prepare financial statements, file tax returns, conduct audits, and help leaders make smart money decisions. A Chartered Professional Accountant (CPA) is Canada's top accounting designation, it's the gold-standard credential that opens the most doors in the field.

$85,000/yr median Outlook: Moderate 6+ years prep
View profile → Business & Finance

Financial Analyst

Financial analysts study companies, markets, and economic data to help businesses and investors make smart money decisions. They build financial models, analyze earnings reports, and recommend investments or business strategies. It is a numbers-heavy, deadline-driven career at the heart of banking, investing, and corporate finance.

$90,000/yr median Outlook: Limited 4–6 years prep
View profile → Business & Finance

Marketing Manager

Marketing managers lead the team that decides how a company presents its products or services to the public. They plan advertising and digital campaigns, set budgets, track results, and manage a team of marketing staff. They work for companies, ad agencies, government departments, and consulting firms, and most reach this role after a few years of hands-on marketing experience.

$119,500/yr median Outlook: Very limited 5–8 years including experience prep
View profile → Business & Finance

Human Resources Specialist

Human Resources Specialists develop, implement and evaluate HR policies and programs, and advise employers and employees on people-related matters. They handle recruitment, onboarding, employee relations, training, compensation and labour relations. They work across nearly every industry, from government and hospitals to tech companies and manufacturers.

$83,000/yr median Outlook: Limited 3–4 years prep
View profile → Business & Finance

Project Manager

A project manager plans, organizes, and leads a project from start to finish, making sure it is completed on time, on budget, and to the required quality. They coordinate the team, manage risks and problems, and keep everyone, from workers to executives, informed. Project managers work in almost every industry, including tech, construction, healthcare, finance, and government.

Salary data unavailable 4–7 years including experience prep
View profile → Business & Finance

Financial Advisor

Financial advisors help individuals and families plan for their financial future, saving, investing, managing debt, and preparing for retirement or estate needs. They assess a client's goals and risk tolerance, then recommend products such as mutual funds, investments, and insurance, earning income through salary, fees, or commission. In Ontario the title is protected by law, so you must hold an FSRA-approved credential or registration to call yourself a Financial Advisor.

$75,000/yr median Outlook: Moderate 4–6 years prep
View profile → Business & Finance

Real Estate Agent

Real estate agents help people buy, sell, and lease homes and commercial properties. They market listings, show properties, negotiate offers, and guide clients through the paperwork of a deal. In Ontario they work as independent contractors under a registered brokerage and are paid by commission, so income depends on closing deals.

$62,500/yr median Outlook: Fair under 1 year (no university degree required) prep
View profile → Business & Finance

Office Administrator

Office administrators keep an office running smoothly behind the scenes. They organize schedules and records, manage supplies and budgets, and help staff and customers get what they need. It is a people-and-organization job found in almost every industry, from clinics and schools to construction firms and government offices.

$61,000/yr median Outlook: Limited 1–2 years prep
View profile → Skilled Trades

Electrician

Electricians install, maintain, and repair the electrical wiring and equipment that powers homes, businesses, and factories. They read blueprints, run wires through walls and conduits, connect panels and fixtures, and troubleshoot problems when the power isn't working.

$70,500/yr median Outlook: Moderate about 5 years prep
View profile → Skilled Trades

Plumber

Plumbers install and repair the pipes, fixtures, and systems that carry water, gas, and waste in homes and buildings. They read blueprints, cut and join pipes, install sinks, toilets, and water heaters, and fix leaks and blockages on service calls.

$67,500/yr median Outlook: Moderate about 5 years prep
View profile → Skilled Trades

Welder

Welders join metal parts together using intense heat from torches, arcs, or lasers. They work from blueprints to cut, shape, and fuse metal for everything from car frames and bridges to custom railings and industrial machinery.

$58,500/yr median Outlook: Very limited about 3 years prep
View profile → Skilled Trades

Carpenter

Carpenters build and repair the wooden (and sometimes steel) structures of buildings, framing walls and roofs, installing doors, windows, floors, and cabinets. They measure, cut, and assemble materials on construction sites and renovation projects.

$66,500/yr median Outlook: Moderate about 4 years prep
View profile → Skilled Trades

Automotive Service Technician

Automotive service technicians, often called mechanics, inspect, maintain, and repair cars and light trucks. They use computerized diagnostic tools to find problems, then fix or replace engines, brakes, transmissions, and electrical systems.

$62,500/yr median Outlook: Moderate about 4 years prep
View profile → Engineering

Mechanical Engineer

Mechanical engineers design and build the machines and systems that move the world, engines, robots, HVAC systems, manufacturing equipment, and more. They use physics, math, and computer modelling to design parts, test prototypes, and make sure equipment works safely and efficiently.

$96,500/yr median Outlook: Limited 4 years to enter the field; about 8 years to P.Eng. prep
View profile → Engineering

Civil Engineer

Civil engineers design and build the infrastructure society depends on, roads, bridges, water systems, transit, and buildings. They plan projects, analyze structural safety, prepare construction drawings, and oversee work on construction sites.

$100,000/yr median Outlook: Moderate 4 years to enter the field; about 8 years to P.Eng. prep
View profile → Education

Elementary School Teacher

Elementary school teachers educate children from kindergarten to Grade 8. They plan lessons, teach core subjects like reading, writing, and math, manage classrooms, assess student progress, and work with parents to support each child's learning.

$96,000/yr median Outlook: Moderate 5–6 years prep
View profile → Legal

Lawyer

Lawyers advise clients on their legal rights and represent them in negotiations, transactions, and court. They research the law, draft contracts and legal documents, argue cases before judges, and help people and businesses solve disputes, from real estate deals to criminal defence.

$135,500/yr median Outlook: Moderate about 8 years prep
View profile → Public Safety

Police Officer

Police officers protect the public by preventing crime, responding to emergencies, and enforcing the law. They patrol communities, answer 911 calls, investigate crimes, arrest suspects, write reports, and testify in court, working in all weather, day and night.

$105,000/yr median Outlook: Moderate under 1 year after being hired prep
